Is my data really private?

Yes—and you can verify it yourself. Open your browser's developer tools while using Cognition and watch the Network tab. You'll see your conversations never leave your browser. All processing happens locally on your device, with AI analysis going directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, bypassing our servers entirely. We architected it this way because we literally cannot access what never touches our infrastructure—no employee access, no data breaches, no government requests possible. When you close the tab, everything vanishes completely. This isn't just a privacy promise; it's simply impossible for us to see your data. Learn more at https://cognition.cv/security.
